## Context

> **To write:** the industry, the scale and who the users were. Describe the client in general terms if you can't name them.

## Challenge

> **To write:** what made this hard: data, scale, latency, safety, accuracy or adoption. Two or three sentences.

## Approach

- Agentic orchestration across multiple models.
- Questions are targeted to the candidate's experience and the job's requirements.
- Built-in question evaluation checks relevance, difficulty and technical depth.
- Each plan includes evaluation criteria, red flags and follow-up questions for the interviewer.

> **To write:** the key decisions and the trade-offs you weighed, at box-and-arrow level. A simple diagram helps: `![Architecture overview](/media/interviewvalley-multi-agent-interviews/architecture.png)`. No code, prompts or internal names.

## Outcome

- A full interview plan in under 3 minutes.

> **To write:** two or three measured results. Rounded numbers or percentages are fine; only claims you can back up.

## Lessons

> **To write:** what you'd tell another team taking on something similar.
